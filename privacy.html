Privacy

Your writing
is yours.

Writed. is designed as a local-first writing application. The application stores your documents on your device and does not require an account or a Writed. server to write.

What Writed. stores

The Writed. application stores projects, chapters, notes, settings, and related writing data locally on your device. The website itself does not receive or store the contents of your writing.

The website

This website may use basic third-party infrastructure required to deliver the page, such as GitHub Pages and Google Fonts. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Downloads

Desktop and mobile application downloads will be provided through the official distribution channels linked from this website. Their respective platforms may collect technical information as described in their own privacy policies.

Contact

If you contact the author by email, the information you provide is used only to respond to your message.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or distribution model changes. The current version will always be available on this page.
